# SportsHedge: shaping a cricket player-trading MVP

Product Management Intern · May to Jul 2024 · Remote

| Interviews | Platforms benchmarked | Influencer partners | Visitors in 2 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30+ | 7 | 10+ | 46k+ |

## The problem

SportsHedge was building a platform where fans trade cricket players. The team needed to decide what the first version should do, and how to bring the first users in.

## Discovery

- **30+ user interviews.** I synthesized them with sentiment analysis and turned the pain points into a single list.
- **7 sports-trading platforms benchmarked.** The comparison showed which features were table stakes and where SportsHedge could stand apart.

## Prioritization

Each pain point became a candidate feature, ranked by how much it hurt users. The benchmark then set the MVP line: match the table stakes, build the differentiators, cut the rest.

## Design

I designed the wireframes and user flows in Figma, aiming for a trading experience a first-time user could follow without help.

## Go-to-market

Launch ran through 10+ influencer partnerships and data-led campaigns, and brought 46k+ visitors in the first 2 months.
