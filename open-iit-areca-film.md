# Areca-nut packaging film: Open IIT Product Management

Team lead of 9 · Oct to Nov 2023 · **1st of 16 teams**

## The brief

Sustainable product innovation: find a product that makes use of crop residue.

## The product

A biodegradable packaging film made from areca-nut shells, a crop residue.

## The case

- **It breaks down.** The film degrades in soil in 78 days.
- **There is a market.** The case projected 22.1% market growth.
- **It fits policy.** The solution aligns with the Self-Reliant India initiative and addresses 10 UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## My role

I led the 9-member team.
